## 3. Detection results

### 3.1 Dark-component search

Thresholding + connected components (area 40–350 px) recovers dozens of candidates per page. The strongest and most spatially consistent clusters appear at:

- Upper-central region ≈ (1150–1225, 626–654)
- Upper-right region ≈ (2110–2170, 646–674)
- Lower-central region ≈ (1147–1224, 2951–2978)
- Various left- and right-margin locations

### 3.2 Cross-page consistency

The upper and lower clusters listed above are **essentially pixel-identical across pages 17, 18 and 19**. This is the same asset-reuse pattern previously established for:
- Spiral ornaments (20–22)
- Plant + side-dot clusters (57 / 68)

### 3.3 Nature of the ornaments

Visual and intensity evidence indicates that the “cross-like ornaments” are **continuous multi-bar line drawings** (Orthodox-style or multi-bar crosses), not discrete circular dots of the kind that form the primary five-dot template.  

Annotation difference maps show labels placed at bar intersections and terminals, consistent with the Master Dataset notes, but do not isolate a small set of large, high-contrast circular marks.

## 5. Interpretation

Pages 17–19 continue the pattern of **high-fidelity graphic asset reuse**. The cross ornaments (and the infinity marks also noted in the Master Dataset) are shared across the three pages with near-pixel identity in key regions.

Because the crosses are continuous line structures rather than discrete point constellations, they do not currently add new high-precision point sets that can be tested against the primary five-dot template via the same signature / rigid-fit pipeline.

This is consistent with the earlier spiral result: many of the visually striking ornaments in *Liber Primus* are reused decorative assets; only a minority present as clean, isolated circular dots.

---

## 6. Recommended geometric next steps

1. Accept the 17–19 crosses as continuous ornaments with confirmed asset reuse; record them in the inventory as such without forcing a discrete point extraction.
2. If discrete points are still desired from these pages, manual seeding of the labelled bar-terminals on one annotated page, followed by local centroid refinement and transfer, remains the most reliable path.
3. Alternative inventory targets that may still yield discrete points:
   - Infinity-symbol endpoints or lobes (pages 17–19 and others),
   - Insect-eye or scale dots noted on page 19,
   - Any remaining unexamined pages that the Master Dataset flags with “dot” or “constellation” language.
4. Update the master workbook with a “Continuous Ornaments / Asset Reuse” sheet summarising 17–19, 20–23 and 57/68.
